About

Academic profile

My academic work examines how AI, data science, and information systems shape knowledge, learning, decision-making, and public trust. I earned a Ph.D. in Information Science with a concentration in Data Science from the University of North Texas and hold a Bachelor of Computer Sciences from Al-Balqa Applied University in Jordan.

My teaching portfolio includes information security, digital forensics, database systems, big data computing and analytics, systems analysis and design, web programming, technical communication, and research literature and techniques. This teaching range supports my broader goal: preparing students to work across computing, cybersecurity, AI, and applied data environments.

  • Current role: Assistant Professor, East Texas A&M University
  • Doctoral training: Information Science, Data Science concentration
  • Core areas: AI, cybersecurity, misinformation, social noise, data analytics, digital learning
  • Editorial role: Associate Editor, Journal of Information & Knowledge Management

Research

Research agenda

My research centers on the design, evaluation, and social impact of AI-enabled systems. The work is organized around three connected questions: how AI changes learning, how AI changes security, and how AI changes public information environments.

AI-driven education

Designing learning environments where educators act as knowledge moderators, guiding students through AI-supported inquiry, feedback, and digital skill development.

CyberAI and workforce preparation

Developing undergraduate pathways that connect cybersecurity, AI, cyber ranges, applied research, and service-oriented workforce preparation.

Misinformation and social noise

Modeling how uncertainty, social noise, and misinformation affect online communication, public understanding, and organizational decision-making.

Responsible AI in health and society

Studying AI bias, clinical decision support, health informatics, and ethical risks in data-intensive systems.

Agentic AI security

Investigating security risks in autonomous AI systems, infrastructure-as-code repair workflows, and AI-enabled cyber harm reporting.

Applied machine learning

Using machine learning, NLP, graph learning, computer vision, and analytics to solve problems in education, health, social media, and cybersecurity.
